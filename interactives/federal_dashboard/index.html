Methodology & Data Classification

Data Source

Data is sourced directly from the USAspending.gov "Spending by Transaction" API. The analysis targets transactions occurring after February 1, 2025, to isolate recent federal administrative actions.

Classification Logic

  • Tier 1 (Confirmed Cuts): Transactions explicitly citing "Rescission," "Sequestration," "DOGE," "Scope Reduction," "EO 14222," or "Radical Transparency." Only these are visualized on the map.
  • Tier 2 (Unexplained): Large de-obligations (>$1M) lacking explanatory keywords. These are flagged for manual review but excluded from dashboard totals to remain conservative.
  • Tier 3 (Routine): Standard closeouts ("unused funds"), administrative corrections, and accounting adjustments.

Exclusions & Safeguards

To prevent false positives, this dashboard excludes:

  • Generic EO Citations: Old grants citing historical Executive Orders are ignored unless they reference specific 2025 directives (e.g., EO 14222).
  • Entitlement Programs: Downward adjustments to Medicaid/Medicare are excluded unless explicitly flagged as "Sequestration" or "Rescission."
