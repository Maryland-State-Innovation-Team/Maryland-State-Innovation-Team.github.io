Methodology & Data Classification

Data Source

Data is sourced directly from the USAspending.gov "Spending by Transaction" API. The analysis targets transactions occurring on or after January 20, 2025, to isolate actions taken during the Trump Administration's second term.

Classification Logic

  • Executive Action (Visualized): Transactions explicitly citing keywords such as "Rescission," "DOGE," "Scope Reduction," "EO 14222," or "Radical Transparency." Only these are shown on the dashboard map and totals.
  • Terminations & Deobligations (CSV Only): Contract cancellations or large withdrawals (>$1M) lacking explanatory keywords. These are included in the full download for transparency but excluded from the dashboard visualization to remain conservative.
  • Routine (CSV Only): Standard administrative closeouts ("unused funds"), accounting adjustments, and clerical corrections.

Exclusions & Context

To ensure accuracy, the visual dashboard applies strict filters:

  • Entitlements: Routine downward adjustments to Medicaid, Medicare, and Social Security are classified as "Routine" and excluded from the dashboard unless explicitly flagged as a policy-driven "Sequestration."
  • Historical Data: Generic citations of older Executive Orders are ignored unless specific 2025 directives are referenced.
