Welcome to The Game of Financial Life!

Navigate the ups and downs of life from age 18 to 65. Make a crucial choice now: will you enroll in a WorkLife Savings Plan (like MarylandSaves) to automatically build an emergency and retirement fund?

How the WorkLife Savings Plan Works:

  • 5% of your income is automatically saved.
  • The first $1,000 goes into a penalty-free Emergency Fund.
  • The rest is invested for retirement in a Roth IRA.
  • You can withdraw your own contributions (not earnings) anytime, tax and penalty-free!
