How This Site Works

No endorsement of any listed businesses/experts

The State of Maryland does not own or endorse any of the third-party businesses or experts listed on this site.

Main features of the Business Compass

The Business Compass relies on OpenStreetMap for locations of known food retail locations. As OpenStreetMap is crowd-sourced, it may contain inaccurate information.

A compilation of state data is used to determine locations of licensed child care providers and display their Maryland EXCELS quality rating. To read more about the Maryland EXCELS program, visit https://marylandexcels.org/.

Main features of “Find Government Funding” section

Grant information is updated on a periodic basis, and is provided solely as a general resource. Third-party requirements for grants change regularly. This means, for example, that new grants may become available that are not listed and others may become unavailable. Similarly, deadlines for grant applications and requirements for eligibility may change.

Main features of “Connect with Experts” section

The "Connect with Experts" page contains publicly available contact information provided by individuals and organizations that support entrepreneurs and small businesses. This information was collected from publicly available third-party websites and may be incorrect or outdated.

The State of Maryland does not own or endorse any of the third-party businesses or experts listed on this site. Users are encouraged to independently verify credentials and qualifications before engaging with providers.

If you would like to submit a correction or request that your information be removed from this page, please email compass@maryland.gov.

Main features of AI-Powered Summaries

This site facilitates easier access to information regarding community planning document contents. The “Community Voice” quotes generated through generative AI, offer qualitative information to supplement quantitative data. These outputs should be read in the context of the original planning documents on which they are based.

  • Sustainable Community applications are sourced from Maryland DHCD.
  • Comprehensive Plans are sourced from Maryland Department of Planning.
  • Baltimore City Community Plans are sourced from the Baltimore City Planning Department.

The State of Maryland provides this feature for convenience and does not endorse or assume responsibility for the content.
