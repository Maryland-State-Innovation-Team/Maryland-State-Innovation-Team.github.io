Impact of SNAP & Medicaid Cuts on Child Poverty

Statewide, proposed cuts for SNAP and Medicaid are estimated to push between 4,500 and 11,000 children (<18) and between 7,500 to 18,000 youth (<25) into poverty in Maryland.

This map shows how these impacts are distributed across Maryland.

How were these numbers estimated?
We used recent Census microdata to identify households with children or youth who would lose SNAP or Medicaid due to proposed work requirements for their caregivers. For SNAP, we subtracted the value of lost benefits from family resources. For Medicaid, we added typical out-of-pocket medical costs for those losing coverage. We then recalculated poverty status for each scenario, using survey weights to estimate the number and percent of children and youth pushed into poverty in each area.
