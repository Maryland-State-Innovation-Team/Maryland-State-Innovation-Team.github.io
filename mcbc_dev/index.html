NourishMD Grant: How to Find the Fresh Food Gap Score

The Maryland Community Business Compass is designed to help entrepreneurs start and grow businesses. If you are a family member looking for child care services, verified safety records, or quality ratings, please use these resources:

1

LOCATE: Child Care (opens in new tab)

Maryland Family Network — A free, personalized service for parents. Use their searchable database or speak with a specialist to find child care that fits your specific needs, location, and schedule.

2

Check Child Care Maryland (opens in new tab)

MSDE — Visit this site to review official licensing information. Verify a provider’s license status, check for verified complaints, and view inspection results to ensure a program meets state safety standards.

3

Maryland EXCELS (opens in new tab)

MSDE — Find programs committed to higher standards of education and care. Locate child care options that have achieved quality ratings to support stronger student outcomes.
