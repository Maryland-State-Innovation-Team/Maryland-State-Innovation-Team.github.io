How it works:

This model first allocates your engagement budget. The employer portion generates newly registered employers, creating a pool of newly eligible employees. The employee engagement budget is then applied to this new pool, reducing their opt-out rate. The final number of savers is the sum of those from the engaged and un-engaged portions of this new employee cohort, added to the program's current baseline.

Baseline assumptions: 108,080 potential employers, 13 employees/employer, 23% base opt-out rate, 15,188 current savers.
